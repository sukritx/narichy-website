Avocado & Mint Foot Cream 90ml

Enjoy some refreshment for hardworking feet. Feel the tingle of naturally cooling peppermint oil invigorate your senses and provide much-needed relief for those tired, aching tootsies.

This moisture-rich foot cream features soothing shea butter and nourishing avocado oil to help smooth and soften skin from heels to ankles, offering easily absorbed, long-lasting hydration.

Ingredients

Certified Organic. 

Aloe Barbadensis Leaf Juice*, Camellia Oleifera Seed Oil*, Glycerin*, Cetearyl Alcohol, Glyceryl Stearate SE, Benzyl Alcohol, Theobroma Cacao (Cocoa) Seed Butter*, Sodium Stearoyl Glutamate, Mentha Arvensis (Peppermint) Leaf Oil, Chondrus Crispus (Carrageenan), Eucalyptus Radiata Leaf Oil*, Butyrospermum Parkii (Shea Butter)*, Cocos Nucifera (Coconut) Oil*, Dehydroacetic Acid, Rosmarinus Officinalis (Rosemary) Leaf Oil*, Citric Acid, Cinnamomum Zeylanicum (Cinnamon) Leaf Oil*, Menthol, Syzygium Leuhmannii Fruit Extract*, Acronychia Acidula Fruit Extract*, Davidsonia Jerseyana Fruit Extract*, Persea Gratissima (Avocado) Oil*, Rosa Canina Fruit Oil*.

*Certified Organic Raw Materials.

Directions

Self-care ritual: Massage a generous amount into skin as required. Do not apply to sensitive areas.

Top tip: Use under cotton socks as a night treatment.

What is Hanbang?

Hanbang is traditional Korean medicine based on natural ingredients with therapeutic properties, a historical practice that remains widely known and used in Korea today.

Our Hanbang-infused skincare combines these time-honored herbs with lab-backed, clinically effective ingredients. 

 

Developed from the historical teachings recorded in the Gyuhap Chongseo, a Joseon-era encyclopedia written by female scholar Yi Bingheogak. Consciously created to care for all skin types.
